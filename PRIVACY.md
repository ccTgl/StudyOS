# StudyOS Beta privacy policy

StudyOS stores your workspace on your device. Workspace data includes notes, PDF annotations, screenshots, tasks, calendars, and study history. The default workspace is `%LOCALAPPDATA%\StudyOS`. You can choose locations for managed files and backups. A full backup can contain personal information from the workspace, including note attachments and cached integration data. Anyone with access to a backup may be able to read it.

Calendar subscriptions contact the feed URL you configure. Optional Google and Microsoft integrations connect to those providers as part of sign-in or sync. Provider privacy terms apply to those communications. OAuth credentials use separate local credential storage and are excluded from ordinary workspace backups.

StudyOS has no hosted cross-device sync service. GitHub hosts the beta downloads and public issue tracker under GitHub's own privacy practices. GitHub Issues is public. Use synthetic examples and leave personal materials and credentials out of your report.

For general questions about this policy, use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/ccTgl/StudyOS/issues/new/choose).
